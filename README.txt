BIRTHDAY WEBSITE - HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (GitHub Pages)

Files
  index.html   the whole website
  photos/      your pictures (1.jpg, 2.jpg, ...)
  music/       your song (munbe-vaa.mp3)

Steps
  1. Create a new repository on GitHub.
  2. Upload everything in this folder: index.html, the photos folder and the music folder.
     (Add your photos and your song first.)
  3. Repo Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root) > Save.
  4. Open the github.io link after a minute or two.

Change the details
  Open index.html in a text editor and find "const PARTY = {" near the top of the script.
  Edit name, passcode, date, place, from and wishes there.
  Also edit AWARDS, CAPSULE (time capsule letter) and PROMISE (promise letter).

Notes
  - Photos and music only load on your github.io link, not inside a chat preview.
  - In a public repo, anyone with the link can see the photos, and the passcode can be
    found in the page code. The login is a simple gate, not real security.
